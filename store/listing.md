## Full Description
CrispChess is a cross-platform chess app with multiple AI engines you can swap at runtime.

ENGINES:
- Built-in: Pure Dart engine (~1800 ELO) — works offline, no download needed
- Maia3: Neural network trained on human games — plays like a real person at your rating level. Choose from 3 model sizes (5M/23M/79M)
- Frozenight: NNUE engine (~3226 ELO) — stronger than any human
- Stockfish: The world's strongest chess engine (downloaded separately)

FEATURES:
- Play as White or Black
- Adjustable opponent strength (20 levels, ~800–2400 ELO)
- Chess clock with 12 time controls (bullet to classical)
- Move hints from the engine
- Opening book with 40+ positions
- PGN export/import
- Board flip
- 8 piece themes
- Animated piece movement with adjustable speed
- Sound effects
- Move history
- Undo support

PRIVACY:
No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your games stay on your device.

OPEN SOURCE:
CrispChess is MIT licensed. Source code at github.com/CrispStrobe/CrispChess
